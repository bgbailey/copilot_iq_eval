## 3. FX Price-Protection Corridors & H2 Discount Trajectory

- **Approved FX Price-Protection Corridors:** Multi-year renewals with +/-4% currency band protection are approved exclusively for **`EMEA`** and **`LATAM`** order forms in FY25.
- **Enterprise Discount Rate Trajectory (`down`):** Average realized enterprise discount rate is targeted **downward** from **`9.8%`** in H1 FY25 to **`7.4%`** in H2 FY25 through tighter CPQ approval thresholds and multi-year commit incentives.

## 4. Retirement of Legacy Per-Seat Overage Fees

The committee approved retiring legacy monthly per-seat overage fees beginning Q3 FY25 and replacing them with **annual pooled capacity commits**. Customer procurement teams reported unpredictable true-up invoices under per-seat overage billing, which slowed renewal velocity by 11 days on average; pooled capacity commits preserve net dollar retention while simplifying burst adoption across business units.
